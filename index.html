Executive Summary

The passage highlights the emergence of commercial space travel pioneered by Virgin Galactic. On December 13, 2018, its suborbital spaceplane, VSS Unity, soared to the edge of space at 51.4 miles (82 km) above sea level. On February 22, 2019, the aircraft took its first passenger, astronaut trainer Beth Moses, into space. She described seeing ice crystals, the curvature of the Earth, the Pacific Ocean, and feeling "infinitely high". With over 600 tourists paying $250,000 each, space tourism gained huge momentum. This initiative was originally inspired by founder Sir Richard Branson's childhood memory of watching the 1969 Apollo 11 moon landing.

India's Private Orbital Milestone

On July 18, 2026, Skyroot Aerospace successfully launched its small satellite launcher, Vikram-1, from ISRO's Satish Dhawan Space Centre. This marked the first time a private Indian company undertook an orbital launch from Indian soil.

Starship's 13th Flight Test

On July 24, 2026, SpaceX lifted off Starship on its thirteenth flight test. This mission deployed 20 next-generation Starlink V3 satellites into orbit and executed a successful soft splashdown.

Roman Space Telescope Fueling

On July 25, 2026, NASA technicians loaded 290 gallons of hydrazine fuel into the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope ahead of its launch to Lagrange point 2 (L2).
